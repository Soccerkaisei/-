How do you write your name in Japanese?

Names from other countries are written in katakana (カタカナ), one of the three Japanese writing systems. Japanese uses sounds like ka, mi, ri, so your name is changed to the closest Japanese sounds. For example, Emily becomes エミリー (Emirī). The line ー makes a sound longer.

What is “jikoshoukai”?

じこしょうかい (jikoshōkai) means self-introduction. Japanese kids start with はじめまして (hajimemashite) — “nice to meet you” — and finish with よろしく おねがいします (yoroshiku onegaishimasu), a friendly way to say “I hope we get along.”
