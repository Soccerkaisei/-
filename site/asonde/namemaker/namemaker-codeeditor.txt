    <h2>How do you write your name in Japanese?</h2>
    <p>Names from other countries are written in <b>katakana</b> (カタカナ), one of the three Japanese writing systems. Japanese uses sounds like <i>ka, mi, ri</i>, so your name is changed to the closest Japanese sounds. For example, Emily becomes エミリー (Emirī). The line ー makes a sound longer.</p>
    <h2>What is “jikoshoukai”?</h2>
    <p><b>じこしょうかい (jikoshōkai)</b> means self-introduction. Japanese kids start with <b>はじめまして (hajimemashite)</b> — “nice to meet you” — and finish with <b>よろしく おねがいします (yoroshiku onegaishimasu)</b>, a friendly way to say “I hope we get along.”</p>
